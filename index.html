Lost Connection

Power is fading in an abandoned factory. Blue can restore energy. Amber can open mechanical gates. Neither can escape alone.

MISSION CONTROL

Restore the connection.

Begin the mission to activate the robots.

  1. Blue: activate the relay to open gate A.
  2. Amber: operate the switch beyond gate A to open gate B.
  3. Bring both robots to the exit.

Stand close to a device to interact. Blue can recharge at either charging pad. Stand close together to transfer energy from the active robot to the other.
